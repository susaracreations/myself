THE PHILOSOPHY

My work is a reaction to the sterilized perfection of modern digital tools. At 16, I look for the gaps where human error meets technical mastery.

Currently in Grade 11 at D.S. Senanayake College, I balance academic structure with the boundless freedom of graphic experimentation. Every project is an attempt to find beauty in the minimalist.
